I've begun to celebrate (slightly) messy human writing.

Reading the newspaper, and noticing two words incorrectly adjoined by a missing space, I feel pleased.

If a piece of work is not perfectly structured, and balanced, with headings and sub headings, I don't mind.

If a phrase seems slightly odd, or out of place, I smile.

If I see the words, "and that's load bearing", I frown.

I feel a little bit more free about:
- informality
- gramatical errors
- spelling errors
- capitilisation
- comma placement
- incorrect apostrophes
- odd phrasings
- local slang
- phonetic spelling
- repetition
- abbreviations
- missing spaces
- etc. 

(That list probably isn't perfect)

Previously, I'd post a message, notice an apostrophe in the wrong place, and edit it. Now I leave it.
